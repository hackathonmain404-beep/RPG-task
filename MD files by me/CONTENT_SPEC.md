# Content Specification

## Brand voice

Confident, playful, motivating, not cringe.

Avoid:
- corporate jargon
- fake hustle language
- over-aggressive motivational slogans

## Public landing page messaging

Core idea:

**Your life is the game. Start gaining XP.**

Supporting idea:
Turn real-world tasks into quests, build streaks, grow attributes and spend earned Gold on your character.

## Suggested public sections

Hero:
- clear promise
- CTA
- interactive product preview

How it works:
- Quest
- Complete
- Level
- Reward

Feature sections:
- RPG progression
- Streaks
- Attributes
- Economy
- Persistent character

FAQ:
Questions should address:
- what is Life RPG?
- how does XP work?
- how do streaks work?
- what are attributes?
- what can Gold buy?
- is my progress saved across devices?

## SEO content rule

Public copy should satisfy user intent first and SEO second.
