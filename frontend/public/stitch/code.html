Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Life RPG is a full-stack gamified productivity platform that transforms daily habits and tasks into a role-playing game. You earn verified XP, level up your character across 5 real-world attributes, maintain streaks, and spend earned currency in a virtual armory.
Quests offer XP calibrated against their difficulty tier (Low, Medium, Hard, Epic). As your XP reaches dynamic thresholds calculated using an exponential scaling formula, your overall Adventurer level and discipline-specific stats automatically level up.
Streaks require at least one qualifying quest completion per 24-hour UTC window. Consistent streaks award cumulative multipliers to earned Gold and attribute bonuses up to a maximum 2.5x multiplier.
The disciplines are Intellect (deep work & technical skill), Strength (physical conditioning & athletics), Wisdom (reading & critical philosophy), Charisma (communication & community), and Vitality (sleep, nutrition & mental recovery).
Gold unlocks cosmetic equipment in the Citadel Armory, custom character avatars, streak-freeze talismans, and personalized real-world milestone unlocks that you configure yourself.
Yes. All user state is persisted directly to our managed PostgreSQL cluster. Sign in on your desktop, mobile browser, or terminal client, and your state will remain completely synchronized in real time.
